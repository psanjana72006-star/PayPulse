How to use TO-DO-List

A quick tour of every page.

🏠 Dashboard

Your day at a glance: what is overdue, what is due today, and your overall progress.

📋 Tasks

Type or speak a task in plain words, like "Submit report tomorrow at 5pm high priority". A preview shows how it was understood.

🗂 Board

Drag cards between To Do, In Progress and Done. Click a card to edit it.

📆 Calendar

See deadlines by month. Click a day to view or add tasks for that day.

⏰ Reminders

Overdue, due today and coming up. Give a task a due time to get an alert before it. Snooze with +1 day.

⏱ Focus

A Pomodoro timer. Pick a task, focus for 25 minutes, and the time is saved to that task.

📈 Progress

Charts of what you finished, how much you focused, and a history of everything you did.

🏆 Achievements

Earn XP, level up and unlock badges for streaks, focus time and finished tasks.

🤖 AI Assistant

Just chat. Say "I need to buy milk tomorrow", "I finished the report" or "I feel overwhelmed". Every change has an Undo button.

Quick tips

  • Press N on any page, or click the round ＋ button, to add a task.
  • Deleted something by mistake? Click Undo in the message that appears.
  • Tasks can have a checklist of subtasks. Progress updates as you tick them.
  • Turn on 🔔 notifications to get reminders even when the tab is in the background.

Questions

The microphone does not work

Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and open the site as http://localhost:3000. Click the 🔒 icon in the address bar and set Microphone to Allow. Voice input also needs an internet connection.

Why does the AI say it is in offline mode?

Offline mode understands everyday sentences about your tasks. For open conversations on any topic, your teacher or you can add an Anthropic API key to the server (see the README).

Where is my data saved?

On the server, in the todo.db file. It is still there after you refresh, log out or restart.
